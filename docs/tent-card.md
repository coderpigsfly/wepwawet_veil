# Tent card: what Wepwawet answers on its own

What she answers with no apps installed. This is the source for the tent card that stands beside a
voice device. When something here changes, the card is made again at the next release to
production. An installed app adds its own section to the card (chores, weather, feast days...).

| Category | Ask her |
|---|---|
| Who's talking | "Who is this?" · "Who am I?" · "Who's talking to you?" |
| Brain fleas: the household's small facts | "Remember that we only buy pizza at Sal's." · "What do you remember?" · "Change the flea about pizza." · "Forget the flea about pizza." |
| What she heard | "What did you hear?" · "What did I say?" · "Who answered that?" |
| Teaching her a miss | "Record that as a misunderstanding." · "That should have been *what you said*." |
| Her questions | "Yes." · "No." · "Read the rest." · "Never mind." |
| Manners | "Good morning." · "Thank you." · "Never mind." · "Go back to sleep." |
| Anything else | Ask it plainly. When home can't answer, she asks around first, or offers to. |

Changing brain fleas by voice is for the people on the Brain fleas page, and only when she's sure
who is speaking.
